# GrailSeeker Support

Need help with GrailSeeker, found a bug, or have a data request?

**Email: andrewkao1230@gmail.com**

We aim to reply within a few business days.

## Common questions

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile tab → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, saved
items, and search history. You don't need to contact us.

**How many searches do I get?**
Guests get 5 per day; signed-in users get 50 per day. The counter resets
daily and is shown in your Profile.

**A listing says "No longer available."**
Listings are provided by eBay and can end or sell at any time. Ended
listings stay visible in your history but can't be opened on eBay.

**Why didn't the app find my item?**
Detection works best on clear, well-lit photos of a single garment. Use
"Search entire image" (or the crop tool when signed in) for accessories like
bags, hats, and shoes.
