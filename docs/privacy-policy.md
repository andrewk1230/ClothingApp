# GrailSeeker Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 15, 2026_

GrailSeeker is a visual search app for second-hand clothing. This policy
describes what data the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete
it.

## What we collect and why

| Data | When | Why | Linked to you? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photos you upload | Every search | Processed on our server to detect garments and find visually similar listings | Only while processing; photos are **not stored** after the search completes |
| Email address and user ID | When you sign in with Google or Apple (via Supabase) | Account sign-in, syncing your saved items and history | Yes |
| Search history (garment category, crop coordinates, result listing IDs) | Signed-in searches | The Search History feature in your Profile | Yes |
| Saved items | When you save a listing | Your wishlist | Yes |
| Hashed IP address | Guest searches | Daily rate limiting (5 free searches/day) | No — stored as a one-way hash, deleted within 7 days |

We do **not** collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or
analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Who else sees your data

- **Supabase** provides authentication (your email and sign-in identity).
- **eBay** provides the listings shown in results. Tapping "View on eBay"
  opens eBay; their [privacy policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-privacy-notice-privacy-policy?id=4260)
  applies there.
- Uploaded photos are processed on our own inference server and are not
  shared with any third party.

We never sell your data.

## Retention and deletion

- Uploaded photos are processed in memory and not retained.
- Search history entries can be deleted individually in the Profile tab.
- **Account deletion:** Profile → Delete Account permanently removes your
  account, saved items, search history, and rate-limit records, immediately.
- Guest rate-limit records (hashed IPs) are purged automatically after 7 days.
